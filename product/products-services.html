Products & Services

Zylohub connects employers with verified workers for short-term physical jobs. Pricing is transparent, in INR, and set by the Hoster based on the job, location, and required manpower.

Core Offering

What Zylohub Offers

Job Matching Marketplace

Hosters post short-term physical work and Seekers apply for the jobs that fit them. The app helps both sides find work faster and hire faster.

  • • Job posting and application flow
  • • Location-based job discovery
  • • Verified worker profiles

Verification & Trust

Workers complete PAN verification and use UPI for payout setup. This keeps the platform more reliable and reduces fake or low-quality profiles.

  • • PAN KYC verification
  • • OTP-based attendance verification
  • • Cashfree-powered payout flow

Pricing Model

Pricing in INR

Seekers

₹0

Free to register, free to apply, and free to receive wages. Seekers do not pay a platform commission.

Hosters

+5%

Hosters pay the worker's agreed wage in INR plus a 5% total fee: 2% Cashfree gateway fee and 3% Zylohub platform fee.

Total charged = worker wage + 5%

Refunds & Cancellations

Policy Details

Non-Refundable After Completion

Once a Hoster initiates payment after job completion and OTP verification, the payment is final and non-refundable.

Cancellation Before Job Start

If a Hoster cancels before a Seeker is confirmed, no payment is involved and no refund is applicable. If a confirmed Seeker does not show up, the Hoster is not charged for that slot.

Platform Fees

The 3% Zylohub platform commission and 2% Cashfree gateway fee are non-refundable once payment has been processed and transferred to the Seeker's UPI account.

Support & Disputes

For genuine technical payment failures or disputes, contact support@zylohub.com within 48 hours. Cases are reviewed individually.

Payment Infrastructure

Powered by Cashfree

Why Cashfree?

Cashfree Payments is a leading Indian payment gateway used for secure UPI payouts and payment processing. Zylohub uses Cashfree to handle payment collection, KYC-related payout setup, and settlement flow.

Zylohub does not store raw payment credentials. Sensitive payment data is handled by Cashfree's secure infrastructure.
