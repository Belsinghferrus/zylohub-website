Step by Step

How Zylohub Works

01

Hoster Posts a Job

A business or individual creates a job listing with type, location, wage per worker, number of seats, and timing. Free to post.

02

Seekers Apply

Nearby workers browse available jobs and send a request to join. Seekers complete PAN card KYC and provide a UPI ID before applying.

03

Hoster Approves Candidates

The Hoster reviews applicant profiles and approves candidates one by one until all seats are filled. Total control over who joins.

04

OTP Attendance Verification

Confirmed Seekers arrive at the job location and verify their attendance using a unique 4-digit OTP held by the Hoster. No-shows are prevented.

05

Job Completion & Payment

After the job is done, the Hoster initiates payment via Cashfree (UPI). Payment goes directly to the Seeker's UPI ID. The Hoster can also review the Seeker's performance.

✓

From post to paycheck

The entire cycle — job posting, application, selection, verification, and payment — happens inside the Zylohub app.

Payment Model

How Payments Work

Agreed job wage Paid to Seeker
Cashfree gateway fee 2%
Zylohub platform commission 3%
Total paid by Hoster Wage + 5%
No payment occurs until the job is physically completed and verified by OTP. All payments are for real-world physical labor services — not digital content.

Cashfree Payments

India's licensed payment gateway processing all wage payouts via UPI. Regulated and compliant under Indian financial laws.

KYC Compliance

All Seekers must complete PAN card verification via Cashfree before applying for jobs — required under Indian financial and labor regulations.

Automatic Payouts

After job completion, Cashfree processes automatic payouts to the Seeker's UPI ID — no delays, no manual transfers.
