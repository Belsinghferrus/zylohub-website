Account & Data Deletion

At Zylohub, you own your data. This page explains exactly how to request deletion of your account and data, what happens after your request, and what we retain for legal compliance.

How to Delete What Happens Next Data Deleted Data Retained Contact
Step-by-Step

How to Request Account Deletion

1
From Inside the App
  1. Open Zylohub app
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap Account → Delete Account
  4. Confirm with your phone OTP
2
Via Email Request
  1. Email support@zylohub.com
  2. Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include your registered phone number or email
  4. We'll confirm and process within 30 days

What Happens After You Request

1

Request Received

We send you a confirmation email/SMS acknowledging your deletion request within 24 hours.

2

Account Deactivated Immediately

Your account is immediately suspended. You cannot log in. Your profile is hidden from all employers and workers on the platform.

3

30-Day Grace Period

Your data is held for 30 days in case you change your mind. You can cancel the deletion by emailing us within this period. No data is permanently deleted during this time.

4

Permanent Deletion

After 30 days, all personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our active systems. Only legally required data is retained (see below).

Data Permanently Deleted

  • Full name, profile photo, bio
  • Email address and phone number
  • Location history and GPS data
  • KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN uploads)
  • In-app messages and chat history
  • Job applications and preferences
  • Ratings and reviews given/received
  • Device info and app usage analytics
Timeline Within 30 days
Legal Requirement

Data Retained After Deletion

  • Transaction records & payment history (7 years — GST Act compliance)
  • Invoice data and payout records (7 years — Income Tax Act)
  • Dispute and complaint records (3 years or until resolved)
  • Fraud/safety flags if flagged account (as required by law)

Retained data is stored securely, anonymized where possible, and used only for legal/compliance purposes. It will never be used for marketing or shared with third parties.

Compliance Indian Law

Active Jobs

If you have active or pending gigs at the time of deletion, you must complete or withdraw them before the account is permanently deleted. Ongoing payments will be settled first.

Changed Your Mind?

You can cancel your deletion request within the 30-day grace period. Simply email support@zylohub.com with subject "Cancel Deletion Request" and your account will be fully restored.

Re-registering Later

After permanent deletion, you may create a new Zylohub account using the same phone number or email. However, your previous history, ratings, and earnings records will not be recoverable.
