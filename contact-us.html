Common Questions

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

  • • General inquiries: 24-48 hours
  • • Support issues: 12-24 hours
  • • Urgent/technical: 6-12 hours
  • • Weekends: Response on next business day

How do I apply for a job at ZyloHub?

Send your resume, portfolio, and a brief cover letter to careers@zylohub.com
Subject line format: "[Position Name] - [Your Name]"
We review applications within 48 hours.

When is ZyloHub launching?

Public launch is Q1 2026 in Chennai. You can join the waitlist now to get early access and be among the first 1,000 workers.

Do you accept corporate partnerships?

Yes! We're open to collaborations with businesses, platforms, and service providers.
Email business@zylohub.com with your proposal.

Can I invest in ZyloHub?

We're currently an early-stage startup and open to conversations with aligned investors. If you're interested, reach out to us with a brief introduction and we'll share more details privately at business@zylohub.com with:

  • • Your background
  • • Investment amount range
  • • Why you believe in our mission

What if I have feedback about the platform?

We'd love to hear it! Email support@zylohub.com with "FEEDBACK:" in the subject line. Your ideas shape our roadmap.

How do I report a bug or technical issue?

Email support@zylohub.com with:

  • • Description of the issue
  • • Steps to reproduce it
  • • Screenshot or video (if possible)
  • • Device/browser info

Do you have a physical office to visit?

Yes, we're based in Chennai. For office visits, meetings, or tours, coordinate at least 48 hours in advance. Most team members work remotely, so advance notice helps us prepare.

Can I speak with a founder?

For serious inquiries (partnerships, investment, press), mention this in your email. We prioritize founder conversations based on relevance and impact.

What are your business hours?

Our business hours are Monday-Friday: 9 AM - 6 PM IST. Saturdays and Sundays are closed, but we provide emergency support when needed. We typically respond to emails within 24 hours even on weekends.

Still have questions? We're here to help!
