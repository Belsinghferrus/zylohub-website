Our Story

zylohub was founded by experienced builders who saw the gap in India's staffing ecosystem. We spent months talking to workers and businesses, understanding the pain points firsthand.

"Agencies steal our money. We want direct jobs."

— Workers told us

"We need reliable workers fast. Agencies are too expensive."

— Businesses told us

We realized: This is a massive market problem that technology can solve.

Today, we're a lean, focused team based in Chennai, building the infrastructure for fair work across India.
