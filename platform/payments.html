Payment Flow

How Payment Works

01

Hoster Sets Job Wage

When posting a job, the Hoster sets the wage per worker in INR. This amount is visible to all Seekers before applying.

02

Seeker Completes KYC

Seekers complete PAN card KYC verification and add their UPI ID through Cashfree before applying for any job. This is a one-time process.

03

Job Completion & OTP Verification

After the job is done, the Seeker's attendance is verified using a unique 4-digit OTP held by the Hoster. No work, no payment.

04

Hoster Initiates Payment

The Hoster initiates the payment inside the Zylohub app. Total amount = agreed wage + 5% fee (2% Cashfree + 3% Zylohub platform commission).

05

Cashfree Processes Payout

Cashfree automatically transfers the full agreed wage to the Seeker's UPI ID. Zylohub deducts only its 3% commission from the transaction.

✓

Seeker Keeps 100%

The Seeker receives the full agreed wage with zero deductions. The 5% fee is always paid by the Hoster on top of the wage.

Policy

Refunds & Cancellations

Job Payments are Non-Refundable

Once a Hoster initiates payment after job completion and OTP verification, the payment is final and non-refundable. This is because payment only happens after the physical job has been completed and confirmed — not before.

Cancellation Before Job Start

If a Hoster cancels a job before any Seeker has been confirmed, no payment is involved and no refund is applicable. If a confirmed Seeker does not show up, the Hoster is not charged for that Seeker's slot.

Platform Fees are Non-Refundable

The 3% Zylohub platform commission and 2% Cashfree payment gateway fee are non-refundable once a payment has been processed and the funds have been transferred to the Seeker's UPI account.

Payment Disputes

In the case of a genuine technical payment failure or error, users may contact support@zylohub.com within 48 hours of the transaction. All disputes are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
