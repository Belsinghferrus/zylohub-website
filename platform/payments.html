Payment Flow

How Payment Works

01

Hoster Sets Job Wage

When posting a job, the Hoster sets the wage per worker in INR. This amount is shown to all Seekers before they apply.

02

Seeker Completes KYC

Seekers complete a one-time PAN card KYC verification and add their UPI ID through Cashfree before applying for any job.

03

Job Completion & OTP

After the job is done, the Seeker's attendance is verified using a unique 4-digit OTP held by the Hoster. No work, no payment.

04

Hoster Initiates Payment

The Hoster initiates payment inside the Zylohub app. Total = agreed wage + 5% fee (2% Cashfree + 3% Zylohub platform commission).

05

Cashfree Processes Payout

Cashfree automatically transfers the full agreed wage to the Seeker's UPI ID. Zylohub only deducts its 3% commission.

Seeker Keeps 100%

The Seeker receives the full agreed wage with zero deductions. The 5% fee is always paid by the Hoster on top of the wage.

Policy

Refunds & Cancellations

Job Payments are Non-Refundable

Once a Hoster initiates payment after job completion and OTP verification, the payment is final and non-refundable. Payment is only triggered after the physical work is completed and verified — not before.

Cancellation Before Job Start

If a Hoster cancels a job before any Seeker has been confirmed, no payment is involved and no refund is applicable. If a confirmed Seeker does not show up, the Hoster is not charged for that slot.

Platform Fees are Non-Refundable

The 3% Zylohub platform commission and 2% Cashfree gateway fee are non-refundable once a payment has been processed and funds have been transferred to the Seeker's UPI account.

Payment Disputes

In the case of a genuine technical payment failure or error, contact support@zylohub.com within 48 hours of the transaction. All disputes are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by our team.
